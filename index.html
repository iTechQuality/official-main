1. Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the following mobile and web application(s) ("App(s)") developed by CHiPS (Chhattisgarh Infotech Promotion Society) as government services. This policy explains how we collect, use, protect, and share your personal information when you use our App(s).

  • CG Geo Survey — Unified multi-department field data collection and GIS mapping platform
  • Government Building Survey
  • PWD Bridge Survey
  • Government EV Charging Station Survey
  • Udyam Aakanksha Certificate — Udyam (MSME) certificate verification & management
  • Know Your Location — Geolocation and mapping services
  • Rescue Portal — Wildlife and emergency rescue management platform
  • Rainfall Monitoring System — Daily rainfall data collection, geofenced verification, and GIS analytics platform

Key Points

  • These App(s) are provided "AS IS" as free government services
  • We collect minimal data necessary for App functionality
  • Location data is used primarily for survey accuracy
  • We do not sell your personal information

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

When you use our App(s), we may collect:

  • Contact Information: Email address (chipsgis97@gmail.com for support purposes)
  • Survey Data: Building/bridge/charging station information, photographs, and survey responses you submit
  • User Credentials: Login information if account creation is required

2.2 Automatically Collected Information

Our App(s) automatically collect certain technical information:

  • Device Information: IP address, operating system, device model
  • Usage Analytics: Pages visited, time spent in App, feature usage
  • Performance Data: Crash reports, error logs (anonymized)

2.3 Udyam Aakanksha Certificate — Additional Data

In addition to the general data above, this App collects:

  • Business Information: MSME/Udyam registration details, business category, and enterprise data submitted during certificate issuance
  • User Credentials: JWT-authenticated login credentials for role-based access
  • Generated Documents: PDF certificates and Excel exports produced on your behalf
  • Location Data: Geolocation used to associate certificates with the correct administrative region

2.4 Know Your Location — Additional Data

In addition to the general data above, this App collects:

  • Precise Location: Real-time GPS coordinates for geolocation and mapping features
  • Map Interaction Data: Search queries, selected points of interest, and map view history
  • Device Sensors: Accelerometer and compass data used to improve location accuracy

2.5 RescuePortal — Additional Data

In addition to the general data above, this App collects:

  • Phone Number: Used for SMS OTP authentication
  • Rescue Case Data: Wildlife/emergency incident details, photographs, and field notes submitted by responders
  • Continuous Location: GNSS-based tracking with sensor fusion (GNSS + IMU) for offline-first field operations; background location may be used during active rescue operations
  • Offline Cache: Map tiles and case data stored locally on-device for use in low-connectivity areas
  • Push Notification Tokens: Device tokens for Firebase Cloud Messaging alerts
  • Web Portal Activity (Admin): Session data and audit logs for the React-based admin dashboard

2.6 Rainfall Monitoring System — Additional Data

In addition to the general data above, this App collects:

  • Precise Location (GPS Geofencing): Real-time GPS coordinates verified against a 20-meter geofence radius around designated rainfall gauge stations before allowing daily data submissions
  • Station & Rainfall Data: Daily rainfall measurements (in millimeters), station metadata, and field operator notes submitted by assigned personnel
  • Operator Credentials & Jurisdiction: JWT-authenticated login credentials, mobile numbers/email, assigned geographic jurisdictions (District ➔ Block ➔ Village ➔ Station), and forced password rotation status
  • Session & Device Tokens: Active device session tokens for single-session control (15-second heartbeat check) to ensure security and prevent concurrent logins across devices
  • Offline Cache: Local SQLite/Hive storage containing queued rainfall entries and cached GIS geography hierarchy (`cg_geography_hierarchy.json` / `cg_block_coordinates.json`) for offline field operations
